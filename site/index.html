an unpublished poem Monsters

one of 35 poems

as the day bleeds,
it growls, comes crippling to me.
as i sit in loneliness,
it seeks pleasure in voicing fear.

it resides under my bed,
at times, in my head.
calling me out, caging me within my trauma,
making it impossible to mobilize.

at dawn it sleeps,
during dusk it preys, cuffing the woeful.
monsters that live under our bed,
immure us, catching hold of us when we’re weak.

these monsters that quiver my soul,
the bane of my existence.
i yearn to overcome them, yet as another night passes, they haunt me,
pulling me off the ground, sucking me from within.

we fight the monsters from our heads,
yet every dusk, they stay under our beds.
crawling to us when we’re most vulnerable,
making these thoughts from us unutterable.

— sanya
